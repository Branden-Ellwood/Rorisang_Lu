Before you order

How do I order?

Add pieces to your bag, check out, and choose how you want to pay. We confirm your order by email and WhatsApp.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The flexible plan splits the total into three equal payments: today, in 30 days and in 60 days. Your order ships after the final payment.

How long does delivery take?

Ready-to-wear pieces ship within 3 to 5 working days. Made-to-measure pieces take longer and we confirm the date before you pay.
